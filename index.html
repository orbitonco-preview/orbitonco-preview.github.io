Together with doctors

How We Partner with Veterinarians

Our partner doctors continue to lead their practices while benefiting from increased case volume, access to advanced technology, and shared infrastructure.

We build long-term partnerships — not acquisitions — with strong clinical alignment

  • You lead the medicine

    Maintain clinical leadership and reputation — your practice, your patients.

  • We build and run the infrastructure

    Operations, systems, hiring, and scalability — so you can focus on medicine.

  • We fund advanced technology

    We invest in equipment that accelerates your clinical capabilities.

  • We grow together

    Long-term alignment with equity participation.

  • Meaningful Equity

    Our doctors become meaningful shareholders of our centers. Combining a vesting and financing structure that significantly reduce their financial risk.

  • Expand your clinical impact

    Increase case complexity and clinical reach without taking on additional operational burden.
